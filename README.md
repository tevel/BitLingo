# BitLingo 🎧

## BitLingo - Learn Languages On The Go | Audio Vocabulary App

### Links

- Website: [bitLingo.app](https://bitLingo.app)
- Blog: [bitLingo.app/blog](https://bitLingo.app/blog) 

**BitLingo** is an audio-first language learning app built for real life: driving, walking, workouts, and any moment when your hands or eyes are busy.  
Instead of flashcards you have to stare at, BitLingo helps you practice vocabulary and phrases through continuous spoken repetition.

Choose a language pair, press play, and learn naturally through listening. BitLingo alternates words and translations with customizable timing, so you can train pronunciation, recall, and comprehension without interrupting your routine.

### What makes BitLingo different

- **Hands-free learning flow** — learn while commuting or multitasking with a simple play/pause experience.
- **Personal dictionary** — create your own word pairs and phrase lists.
- **Fast input options** — add words by typing, file import, or image text extraction (OCR).
- **Two-way practice** — switch learning direction (Language A → B or B → A).
- **Natural audio practice** — uses text-to-speech plus support for custom audio on saved words.
- **Categories + custom lists** — study built-in phrase groups or fully personalized vocabulary.
- **Smart repetition controls** — tune delays and playback behavior to match your pace.
- **Archive workflow** — keep active study sets clean while preserving past items.
- **Progress & motivation** — session stats and achievement-style milestones.
- **Mobile-friendly experience** — optimized for on-the-go use, including installable web app support.

### Ideal for

- Commuters who want to learn without screen time
- Busy learners who prefer listening over tapping through cards
- Travelers preparing practical phrases quickly
- Anyone building a personalized vocabulary routine

## Features

- 📱 **Progressive Web App** - Install on any device, works offline
- 🔊 **Text-to-Speech** - Hear words in native pronunciation
- 🎤 **Audio Recording** - Record your own pronunciations
- 🔄 **Spaced Repetition** - Auto-archive mastered words after 50 plays
- 🔥 **Daily Streak** - Track your 30-minute daily learning goal
- 💪 **Persistent Student** - Weekly consistency tracking
- 🎉 **Weekly Words** - Track words mastered each week
- 🌙 **Dark Theme** - Beautiful cosmic-inspired dark design
